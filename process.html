  1. Free intro call

    • No cost and no obligation
    • Phone, video or in person

  2. Plan & price

    Within [N] days of our call

    I send a short written plan: the pages, the features and a fixed price. Nothing starts until you say yes.

    • Who owns the domain and hosting (you do)
    • Timeline and payment schedule

    About a week

    You send what you have, and I fill in the rest. I can write the words and take or find photos if you need.

    About [N] weeks

    I design and build the site and send you a private link so you can see it on your own phone.

    • Built so Google can read it

    Up to [N] rounds of changes

    We go through it page by page. You tell me what to change, and I change it.

    • Words, photos, colors and layout
    • Changes after launch are covered by the care plan or billed hourly

    Launch day

    Your site goes live on your domain. I connect it to your Google listing and show you how to make small updates yourself.

    • Domain and email connected
    • Google Business Profile linked

    Optional, monthly

    If you'd rather not deal with it, the care plan covers hosting, updates, backups and small changes.

    • Cancel anytime
